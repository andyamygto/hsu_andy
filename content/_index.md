I am a computational condensed matter physicist at the University of Southern California,
working with Zhenglu Li. I use first-principles many-body methods
(GW, the Bethe–Salpeter equation, and GW perturbation theory) to study electron–phonon
coupling, superconductivity and charge order in quantum materials.
Before USC I was at Tamkang University in Taiwan, working with Hung-Chung Hsueh.

This site also holds my notes on methods and calculations.
